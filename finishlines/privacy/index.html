Quietly Useful LLC · Finish Lines

Privacy

Finish Lines keeps a record of the races you have run. The record is yours. It stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, and nothing about it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app collects

Nothing. Finish Lines has no account to sign up for, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests of its own. There is no server at Quietly Useful that your data could reach.

Where your record lives

  • On your iPhone, in the app's own storage.
  • In your iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on for the app. Sync uses Apple's CloudKit, in the private database that belongs to your Apple Account. Quietly Useful cannot read it, and Apple's iCloud terms govern it.
  • In backup files the app writes to its own iCloud container, so an earlier copy of your record can be restored. These are your files, in your account.

Files you bring in or take out

Importing a spreadsheet reads the file you choose and nothing else. Exporting writes a spreadsheet or a backup to a place you choose. The app never uploads either anywhere.

App lock

If you turn on App lock, the app asks iOS to confirm it is you with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. That check happens inside iOS. The app never sees your face, fingerprint, or passcode — only an answer of yes or no.

Links you add to a race

You can attach a results link to a race. Opening it hands the address to your browser, the same as tapping a link anywhere else. The app sends nothing along with it.

Deleting

A deleted race is kept in the app's Deleted races list until you remove it, so a mistake can be undone. Erasing the archive removes every race from your iPhone and from your iCloud private database. Backups you have made stay in your iCloud container until you delete them.

Children

Finish Lines is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.
